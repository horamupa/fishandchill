How to check if an iPhone game works offline

Search the App Store description for words like offline, no Wi-Fi or no internet. Developers who support offline play usually say so, because players look for it.

Then test it before you leave: open the game once while you're online so any extra content finishes downloading, turn on Airplane Mode and launch it again. If it starts and lets you play, you're set. Some games run offline but only sync progress or leaderboards once you reconnect.

Questions about offline iPhone games

What are the best offline games for iPhone?

It depends on the mood. For something calm, try Fish & Chill or Puzzledom. For strategy, The Battle of Polytopia. For action, Soul Knight, Endless Wander or Brotato. For quick arcade sessions, Hill Climb Racing, Crossy Road, Temple Run 2 or Block Blast.

Are there free offline games for iPhone?

Yes. Nine of the ten games on this list are free to download: Fish & Chill, The Battle of Polytopia, Hill Climb Racing, Soul Knight, Endless Wander, Puzzledom, Block Blast, Crossy Road and Temple Run 2. Brotato: Premium costs a one-time price.

Can I play these games on a plane?

Yes. Download them before the flight, open each once while online, then turn on Airplane Mode. All ten say in their App Store description that they play offline.

Do offline games save my progress?

Yes, progress is saved on your iPhone. Some games, like Endless Wander and Brotato, also sync to the cloud, but only when you're back online.
