Zen games: 10 meditative games for iPhone in 2026

Zen games are games with no timer, no enemies and nothing to lose. You repeat one small action, the screen answers slowly, and after a few minutes your head is quieter. These ten zen games for iPhone work that way: fishing, koi, color puzzles, town building and plain sand.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest. Every other app here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. We picked games with no timer at the core, or with a mode you can't lose, rated 4.5 or higher and on sale in the US App Store in November 2026. Thisissand is listed under Entertainment, not Games; we kept it because it is the purest toy here. Apple Arcade and Netflix games are left out because they need a subscription.

What is a zen game?

A zen game is a game built around a calm, repeatable action and no fail state: no timer, no enemies, no lives, often no score. You play for the process itself. The name is a loose borrowing. These games have nothing to do with Zen Buddhism, and playing them is not meditation.

Zen games overlap with cozy and relaxing games, but they are barer. A cozy game gives you a farm, neighbors and a to-do list. A zen game gives you one thing to do, such as sorting colors, placing blocks or waiting for a fish, and leaves you alone with it.

How to pick a zen game

Pick by what your hands want to do. For sorting and tidying: I Love Hue, Unpacking or Empty. For building with no goal: Townscaper, Thisissand or Mini Metro in Endless mode. For slow watching and waiting: Zen Koi 2 or Fish & Chill.

Check two things before you buy. First, whether the calm part is the default or a separate mode, as in Mini Metro. Second, whether the game counts anything. KAMI 2 counts your moves, and some people can't relax while a counter is running.

Questions about zen games

What are zen games?

Games with a calm, repeatable action and no way to fail: no timer, no enemies, usually no score. On iPhone that means things like I Love Hue, Unpacking, Townscaper, Zen Koi 2 and Fish & Chill.

Are there free zen games for iPhone?

Yes. Six of the ten are free to download: Fish & Chill, Zen Koi 2, I Love Hue, KAMI 2, Empty. and Thisissand. Some offer optional in-app purchases. Unpacking, Townscaper, Mini Metro and Hidden Folks are paid.

Are zen games the same as meditation?

No. A zen game can slow you down for a few minutes, and many people like that. It is still a game, not a meditation practice and not a treatment for stress or anxiety.

Is there a zen game for Apple Watch?

Yes. Fish & Chill plays fully on Apple Watch: you cast with a tap and reel by turning the Digital Crown, with haptics on every bite. The other apps on this list are made for iPhone.
