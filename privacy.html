Privacy Policy

Fish & Chill
Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Fish & Chill app for iPhone and Apple Watch ("the App", "we") handles your information. The short version: we do not collect your name, email, location, or any other personal details. To improve the game, the App sends anonymous gameplay statistics. They are not linked to your identity, not used for advertising, and not used to track you.

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous gameplay statistics: game events (for example, the first cast, upgrades bought, screens opened, purchase attempts) and a progress summary (number of fish caught, species discovered, deepest zone reached, line length, coins, casts, PRO status). They are tied to a random identifier created by the analytics service, not to your Apple ID, name, or email.
  • Crash reports on iPhone: technical details about the crash and your device model and system version.
  • Feedback you choose to send: the text and rating you enter and, only if you type it, your email address.

The App does not require registration or an account, does not access your location, contacts, photos, health data, or microphone, contains no advertising, and never asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Game Data

All game progress (caught fish, coins, upgrades, daily goals, and settings) is stored locally on your device. Only the anonymous statistics described above leave the device. Deleting the App permanently deletes your local progress.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (PRO features) processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive or store your payment information, billing details, or Apple ID. Purchases are governed by Apple's own privacy policy, available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Cookies

The App does not use cookies.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (iPhone): gameplay statistics and crash reports. Firebase privacy.
  • PostHog (Apple Watch): gameplay statistics. PostHog privacy.
  • Google Forms: delivers the feedback you choose to send. Google privacy.
  • Apple App Store: processes in-app purchases, as described above.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The statistics it sends are anonymous and describe gameplay only.

Security

Statistics are sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Game progress on your device is protected by your device's built-in security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
