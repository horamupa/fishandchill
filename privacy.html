Privacy Policy

Fish & Chill
Last updated: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Fish & Chill app ("the App", "we") handles your information. The short version: we do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App:

  • does not require registration or an account;
  • does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal details;
  • does not access your location, contacts, photos, health data, or microphone;
  • does not contain analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Game Data

All game progress (caught fish, coins, upgrades, daily goals, and settings) is stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or to any third party. Deleting the App permanently deletes this data.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (PRO features) processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive or store your payment information, billing details, or Apple ID. Purchases are governed by Apple's own privacy policy, available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Cookies

We do not use cookies.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or external servers. The only external interaction is with Apple's App Store for processing in-app purchases, as described above.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children. It is safe to use at any age.

Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data at risk. Game progress is protected by your device's built-in security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
