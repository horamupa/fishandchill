REAL TIME

A lake that keeps your hours

Dawn, day, dusk and night follow your actual clock — no fake day cycles. The sun and moon cross the sky in real time, and every species keeps its own hours: miss the dusk bite, wait for tomorrow's.

REEL

Reel with your finger — or your Crown

On iPhone you hold the rod, watch the tension and drag the ReelPad to fight the fish. On Apple Watch you crank the Digital Crown while haptic ticks count every meter of line you win back.

DIVE

Go deeper. That's the whole progression

Legendaries hide in the dark. Upgrade your line from 10 m all the way to 200 m, pick the right bait — from plain Bread for the shallows to Glowworm for the midnight deep — and sink past the easy fish.

COLLECT

Twenty species to outsmart

7 common, 6 rare, 4 epic and 3 legendary fish. Each one gets a Fishdex card with its depth, hours and favorite bait — so every empty slot is a puzzle, not a grind.

LAND

Reel smart, not fast

Let the line scream in the red zone too long and it snaps. Fights are won by feel: give line when the fish runs, crank when it tires. A fail is always explainable — never just bad luck.

Questions

Is Fish & Chill free?

The game is free to download and play, with an optional one-time PRO purchase. There are no ads and no energy timers.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The game needs no internet and no account — everything is stored on your device. Perfect for flights and dead zones.

Can I play on Apple Watch without my iPhone?

Yes. The full game runs on the watch: you cast with a tap and reel with the Digital Crown. The iPhone version is a companion, not a requirement.

When do fish bite?

Each of the 20 species keeps its own real-world hours. Some bite at dawn, some only after dark — the in-game water follows your actual clock, so check it at different times of day.

How deep can I fish?

You start with a 10 m line and can upgrade to 200 m. Rare, epic and legendary species live deeper, and the deepest ones only bite on the right bait.
