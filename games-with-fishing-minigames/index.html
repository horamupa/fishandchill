Games with fishing minigames: 10 iPhone games where you can fish in 2026

Games with fishing minigames on iPhone include Stardew Valley, Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete, Sea of Stars, Old School RuneScape, Albion Online and Heartopia. In each of them fishing is a side activity next to farming, questing or decorating: a quiet break with a rod before the main game calls you back. This list covers ten, including one game where the minigame is the whole thing.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. It is also the odd one out: fishing is not a minigame there, it's the entire game. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one names fishing on its own App Store page, plays on iPhone, was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026 and is rated 4.4 or higher. Some famous games with fishing are missing because their store pages don't mention it, and we only repeat what a store page confirms.

  1. 1. Fish & Chill

    Fishing, start to finish · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.8 (19)

    What you get when the fishing minigame becomes the whole game. The lake follows your real clock and fish keep their own biting hours, so the evening water holds different fish than the morning. You upgrade the line from 10 to 200 m and move from bread to glowworm bait. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and on Apple Watch you reel with the Digital Crown.

    Download on the App Store

  2. 2. Stardew Valley

    Farming RPG · Paid · ★ 4.8 (64K)

    A farming RPG where fishing is one of many ways to spend a day. Its App Store page offers a relaxing afternoon at the local fishing spots or crabbing by the seaside, next to crops, animals, caves full of monsters and seasonal festivals, and a later update added fishing ponds. It's a one-time purchase, rebuilt for touch with auto-save.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete

    Camp life · Paid · ★ 4.8 (9.9K)

    You manage a campsite, and between decorating it and chatting with the animals you fish and catch bugs. Fishing Tourneys are among the events held every month. This is the one-time-purchase edition of Pocket Camp: seven years of items and events, with no additional in-game purchases.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Sea of Stars

    Turn-based RPG · Paid · ★ 4.4 (228)

    A retro-styled RPG about two Children of the Solstice, with turn-based combat and no random encounters. Between battles the world gives you sailing, cooking, fishing and a tabletop game called Wheels at the tavern. A paid game with an interface redesigned for touch.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. Old School RuneScape

    MMORPG · Free · ★ 4.8 (94K)

    Fishing here is not a five-minute break but one of 24 skills you train for the long run, alongside Combat, Runecrafting and the newer Sailing. The game is a fantasy MMORPG based on the 2007 version of RuneScape, and new content is voted in by its players. Free to download. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Albion Online

    Sandbox MMORPG · Free · ★ 4.8 (23K)

    A cross-platform sandbox MMORPG with a player-driven economy, where nearly every item is crafted by players. Across five biomes you gather raw materials or cast a line into Albion's waters to catch fish. It also has full-loot fights between players, so this is the least quiet game on the list.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. Heartopia

    Life sim · Free · ★ 4.6 (37K)

    A life sim where fishing is one of six hobbies, together with cooking, gardening and birdwatching. Its store page puts it simply: hobbies are yours to enjoy, not chores. Around them you build a house, pick from over 1,000 outfits, adopt cats and dogs and drop by friends' places.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. Tsuki's Odyssey

    Passive adventure · Free · ★ 4.5 (9.5K)

    A passive adventure about Tsuki and the oddball residents of Mushroom Village. You decorate the home, make friends and catch all kinds of fish. Tsuki is a free spirit who moves around as they please, so this is a game you check in on, not one you play for hours.

    View on the App Store

  9. 9. Black Desert Mobile

    Action MMORPG · Free · ★ 4.6 (33K)

    An action MMO with 14 classes and fast combat. Fishing and taming belong to what the store page calls life content, together with a Camp of your own to manage and expand. The page lists iPhone 12 Pro as the device requirement and notes that the game includes random-item purchases.

    View on the App Store

  10. 10. The Legend of Neverland

    Anime MMORPG · Free · ★ 4.5 (8K)

    An anime-style MMORPG where you fight alongside flower fairies and switch between classes such as swordsman, ranger, scholar and craftsman. Outside battle, the store page offers fishing, cooking and building your own homeland.

    View on the App Store

Questions about games with fishing minigames

What games have fishing minigames?

On iPhone: Stardew Valley, Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete, Sea of Stars, Old School RuneScape, Albion Online, Heartopia, Tsuki's Odyssey, Black Desert Mobile and The Legend of Neverland all name fishing on their App Store pages. Fish & Chill is fishing only.

Which game has the best fishing minigame?

It depends on what you want from it. For a break between farm chores, Stardew Valley. For a hobby among other hobbies, Heartopia. For a skill you level over months, Old School RuneScape. If the fishing is the part you like most, try a game built only around it, like Fish & Chill.

Are there free games with fishing minigames on iPhone?

Yes. Old School RuneScape, Albion Online, Heartopia, Tsuki's Odyssey, Black Desert Mobile and The Legend of Neverland are free to download, and some offer in-app purchases. Fish & Chill is free with an optional one-time PRO purchase.

What if I only want the fishing part?

Then skip the minigame and play a fishing game. Fish & Chill is one lake that follows your real clock: fish bite at their own hours, the line grows from 10 to 200 m, and there are no ads or energy timers. It works offline.

Can you play a fishing game on Apple Watch?

Yes. Fish & Chill runs fully on Apple Watch without the iPhone nearby. You reel with the Digital Crown and feel the fish through haptics. It needs watchOS 11 or later.
