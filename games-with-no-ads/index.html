How to tell if an iPhone game has ads before you download it

Read the description first: games without ads usually say so, because it's a selling point. Then scroll to In-App Purchases. An item called Remove Ads or No Ads means the free version shows them.

Check App Privacy on the same page too. If the developer lists Third-Party Advertising or data used to track you, expect ads. A one-time price and no in-app purchases is the safest sign of a game with no ads at all.

Questions about games with no ads

Are there free games with no ads?

Yes. Fish & Chill, Sky: Children of the Light, Bullpen, Chess without ads and Solitaire Collection are free to download and say they show no ads. Monument Valley 3 and A Little to the Left are free to try and unlock the full game with one purchase, also without ads.

Are there offline games with no ads?

Yes. Fish & Chill, Bullpen, Chess without ads and Solitaire Collection work without internet and show no ads. Sky is an online game.

Why do most free games have ads?

Ads pay for free games. Games without ads earn money another way: an upfront price, a one-time unlock, or optional cosmetic purchases.
