Games like Stardew Valley: 10 picks for iPhone in 2026

Looking for games like Stardew Valley on your phone? The closest match on iPhone is Harvest Town, a free pixel farm RPG with seasons, villagers and marriage. Farm RPG goes deeper on crafting and fishing, Pocket Camp Complete swaps the farm for a campsite full of animals, and Fish & Chill keeps just the quiet hours by the water.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one shares the Stardew Valley loop of growing, raising, fishing or building a little home, is rated 4.5 or higher and was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026. Apple Arcade and Netflix games are left out because they need a subscription.

  1. 1. Fish & Chill

    Calm fishing · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.8 (17)

    For players whose favorite part of Stardew Valley was the fishing. The lake follows your real clock: dawn, day, dusk and night, and each of the 20 species bites at its own hours. You upgrade the line from 10 to 200 m and work through baits from bread to glowworm. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and the whole game also runs on Apple Watch, where you reel with the Digital Crown.

    Download on the App Store

  2. 2. Harvest Town - Pixel Sim RPG

    Pixel farm RPG · Free · ★ 4.8 (19K)

    The nearest thing to Stardew Valley on mobile: clear the weeds, fix up your cottage, raise chickens, ducks and cattle, and adopt a cat or a dog. Four seasons turn over, villagers have their own stories, and you can court and marry one of them. Caves and hidden treasure chests wait for the curious. It also has online features like market trading and races with other players.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. Farm RPG

    Menu-based farm MMO · Free · ★ 4.9 (13K)

    A farming RPG played through menus instead of a walking character, with years of content behind it. You grow crops, fish with different baits across many spots, mine, cook, craft and raise animals, and later start a vineyard and wine cellar. The App Store page promises no ads and no competitive pressure, and a friendly online community around it.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete

    Campsite life · Paid · ★ 4.8 (9.9K)

    The one-time purchase version of Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp, with seven years of items and events and no extra in-game purchases. You run a campsite, fish, catch bugs, chat with animal neighbors and decorate with more than 10,000 items. The slow Animal Crossing rhythm, at a price paid once.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. Slime Rancher

    Ranching adventure · Paid · ★ 4.8 (1.8K)

    Beatrix LeBeau moves to a ranch a thousand light years from Earth and gets it running again by catching and raising slimes. Each slime type has its own likes and needs, and keeping them happy is the farm work here. The App Store page lists a few older devices with limited RAM that may freeze or crash.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Cat Town Valley: Healing Farm

    Cat village farm · Free · ★ 4.8 (3.3K)

    A village farm where every neighbor is a cat with its own personality and skills. You grow wheat, corn and pumpkins, chop wood, build and upgrade houses, and help residents with quests. The App Store page itself pitches it to fans of Stardew-style games.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. Tiny Harvest: Cozy Idle Farm

    Idle farm · Free · ★ 4.8 (415)

    A cozy idle farming game for days when you only have a few minutes. Crops grow on simple timers, you craft goods, unlock new areas and slowly build your own valley. Crops don't rot while you're away, and the developer says there are no forced ads and no timers that punish breaks.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. Hay Day

    Classic farm sim · Free · ★ 4.7 (656K)

    The long-running farm game from Supercell, with more than 650,000 ratings in the US App Store. Grow crops that never die, raise animals, go fishing, run a bakery or sugar mill, and trade with neighbors or fill truck orders. More business than story, but the daily farm routine is all there.

    View on the App Store

  9. 9. Family Island — Farming game

    Island town builder · Free · ★ 4.8 (422K)

    A Stone Age family of four, Bruce, Eva and their kids, lands on a remote island after a volcano wipes out their old home. You farm, gather, explore new islands and build them a town, with plenty of family humor along the way. The App Store page calls it an offline adventure game.

    View on the App Store

  10. 10. My Dear Farm

    Kawaii farm · Free · ★ 4.6 (12K)

    A small, cute farm sim from HyperBeard, the studio behind Pocket Love and Tsuki's Odyssey. Customize your farmer and your pet, grow crops, sell your harvest at the market, and unlock furniture and decoration sets to style the farm the way you like.

    View on the App Store

Pick by what you loved in Stardew Valley

If it was the whole package of farm, seasons and villagers, start with Harvest Town. If it was the long grind of crops, crafting and fishing, Farm RPG has years of it. If it was the neighbors and decorating, Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete or Cat Town Valley. If you want a farm with a strange twist, Slime Rancher.

Short on time? Tiny Harvest and Hay Day work in five-minute visits. And if your best Stardew days were spent at the pier, Fish & Chill is fishing and nothing else, on a lake that follows your real clock.

Ratings: US App Store, November 2026.

Questions about games like Stardew Valley

What games are like Stardew Valley on iPhone?

Harvest Town is the closest: a pixel farm RPG with seasons, villagers and marriage. Farm RPG, Cat Town Valley and Tiny Harvest cover the farming, Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete the cozy village life, Slime Rancher a stranger kind of ranch, and Fish & Chill the fishing.

Is Stardew Valley on iPhone?

Yes. Stardew Valley is in the US App Store for $4.99 as a one-time purchase, rated 4.8 from about 64,000 ratings in November 2026. Apple Arcade subscribers can also play Stardew Valley+.

Are there free games like Stardew Valley?

Yes. Harvest Town, Farm RPG, Cat Town Valley, Tiny Harvest, Hay Day, Family Island, My Dear Farm and Fish & Chill are free to download. Most offer optional in-app purchases.

Are there games like Stardew Valley without ads?

Farm RPG says on its App Store page that it has no ads, and Tiny Harvest promises no forced ads. Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete is a one-time purchase with no extra in-game purchases. Fish & Chill has no ads and no energy timers.

Is there a Stardew Valley style game for Apple Watch?

Not a farming one. Fish & Chill is the closest in spirit: a cozy fishing game that runs fully on Apple Watch, with a day and night lake that follows your real clock.
