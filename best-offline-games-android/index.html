How to check if an Android game works offline

Open the game's Google Play page, read the full description and look for words like offline, no Wi-Fi or no internet. Developers who support offline play usually say so, because players search for it. Then check the labels under the title, which show whether the game contains ads or in-app purchases.

Test it before you leave. Open the game once on Wi-Fi so any extra content finishes downloading, turn on airplane mode, and start it again. If it reaches gameplay, you're set. Events, leaderboards and multiplayer usually still need a connection.

Questions about offline Android games

What is the best offline game for Android?

It depends on what you like. For a calm session, try Cozy Fishing. For strategy, Bloons TD 6 and The Battle of Polytopia are safe picks. For a long adventure, Vampire's Fall: Origins is a full RPG that needs no internet.

Are there free offline games for Android?

Yes. Nine of the ten games here are free to download; only Bloons TD 6 is paid. Cozy Fishing is free down to 10 m, and most of the others offer optional in-app purchases.

Which offline Android games have no ads?

Cozy Fishing has no ads. Bloons TD 6, The Battle of Polytopia and Iron Marines carry no ad label on Google Play. Google Play marks the other six games here as containing ads.

Do offline games work in airplane mode?

Yes, as long as the game is installed and any extra content has finished downloading. Open it once on Wi-Fi before your trip to be sure.

Do Android games use mobile data when offline?

A game with no connection can't use any data. If you leave mobile data on, a game may still go online for ads or updates, so airplane mode is the simple way to rule that out.
