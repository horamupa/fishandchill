Games to play on a plane: 10 iPhone games that work in airplane mode

The best games to play on a plane are the ones that need no Wi-Fi and last more than a few minutes. Each of these ten says in its App Store description that it plays offline or with a friend nearby over Bluetooth or on one phone. Download them before you board, open each once, and a long flight gets much shorter.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. How to get games ready before a flight

Download every game at home, open each one while you're still online and let any extra content finish downloading. Then turn on Airplane Mode and launch them again. If a game starts and lets you play, it will work in the air.

Charge your phone fully and pack a cable: most seats have USB power, but not all of them work. If you want to play with a seatmate over Bluetooth, you can switch Bluetooth back on after enabling Airplane Mode, where the airline allows it.

Questions about games to play on a plane

What games can you play on a plane without Wi-Fi?

Any game that runs offline. On this list: Fish & Chill, Bloons TD 6, Kingdom Rush, World Conqueror 4, Eternium, OneBit Adventure, Sudoku and Solitaire all say in their App Store description that they play offline.

Can you play games in airplane mode?

Yes, as long as the game doesn't need a server. Open it once before the flight so extra content downloads, then test it in Airplane Mode at home.

What can I play on a plane with a friend?

Sea Battle 2 plays over Bluetooth or on one phone, and Two Spies plays over local wireless or by passing one phone back and forth.

Which games last a long flight?

Deep strategy and RPGs: Bloons TD 6, Kingdom Rush, World Conqueror 4 and Eternium can fill hours. Fish & Chill, Sudoku and Solitaire are better for short, calm sessions.

Are these games free?

Nine of the ten are free to download. Bloons TD 6 costs a one-time price. Some free games offer optional in-app purchases.
