One handed games for iPhone: 9 picks for 2026

The best one handed games on iPhone are built for portrait mode and a single thumb. One More Brick is a free brick breaker with one-thumb controls, holedown digs to planet cores one shot at a time, and Meteorfall: Krumit's Tale is a full roguelike deckbuilder you can play one-handed. All nine say so on their App Store page.

We make Fish & Chill, a cozy fishing game for Apple Watch and iPhone. It isn't on this list because we only included games whose App Store page promises one-handed or one-finger play. All nine games come from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one is rated 4.3 or higher with at least 200 ratings and was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026. Casino and video poker apps are left out.

  1. 1. One More Brick

    Brick breaker · Free · ★ 4.6 (7.4K)

    Aim with your thumb, fire hundreds of balls at once and watch the bricks shatter. Power-ups like the Ball Duplicator set off chain reactions, and there are Endless and Levels modes. The App Store page calls it ideal for playing one handed, with one-thumb controls, and it works offline.

    View on the App Store

  2. 2. holedown

    Ball-bouncing digger · Paid · ★ 4.3 (399)

    Shoot balls into blocks to dig toward the core of six planets. Shots per round are limited, so every angle counts, and crystals pay for upgrades like more balls or more shots. About five hours of main game plus an endless mode, with intuitive one-handed play according to the developer. It costs $3.99 once.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. POINPY

    Vertical climber · Free · ★ 5.0 (294)

    From the creator of Downwell, published by Devolver. You bounce upward, flip around and grab fruit for the blue beast chasing you from below. The controls are one-handed, runs are randomly generated, and finishing the game unlocks an endless mode.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Meteorfall: Krumit's Tale

    Roguelike deckbuilder · Paid · ★ 4.5 (267)

    Pick one of five heroes and swipe cards on a grid to fight through dungeons. More than 300 cards, 50 perks and a daily challenge with changing rules. The game plays one-handed in portrait mode, auto-saves often, works offline and syncs through iCloud. A one-time $6.99.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. Cards of Terra

    Solitaire-style card game · Free · ★ 4.8 (425)

    A single-player card game that mixes solitaire-like play with collectible card mechanics. You drag enemy cards around so they fight each other and clear your path. 80 handcrafted levels, 9 bosses and a draft mode. It's designed for one-handed play and works offline, and one purchase removes the ads. The last update dates from 2022.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Hexascapes - Block Puzzle

    3D tap puzzle · Free · ★ 4.8 (8.7K)

    Every hexagonal block has an arrow. Tap it and the block flies off that way, unless something is in the way. Peel the 3D shape apart layer by layer without jamming the exits. The developer describes the gameplay as easy and one-handed, and there's a daily challenge mode.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. Nuts And Bolts - Screw Puzzle

    Screw puzzle · Free · ★ 4.5 (33K)

    Unscrew bolts and move them between holes to free tangled metal plates. There's no time limit, levels come in easy and hard modes, and later boards add saws and bombs. Everything is done with one finger, as the App Store page puts it.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. Hole It 3D

    Black hole puzzle · Free · ★ 4.7 (32K)

    Drag one finger to steer a black hole around a colorful map and swallow only what the mission asks for, such as all the red cars or just the donuts. The hole grows with every object, and the challenge is choosing what to eat. Controls are one-finger by design.

    View on the App Store

  9. 9. FallDown! III

    Endless roller · Free · ★ 4.4 (235)

    A remake of the classic FallDown: keep your ball dropping through gaps before the floor pushes it off the top. There's an action-packed Arcade mode and a calmer Zen mode, plus daily challenges. Single-finger touch controls let you play with one hand. The last update dates from 2020.

    View on the App Store

How to pick a one-handed game

Want something calm? The puzzles fit best: Hexascapes, Nuts And Bolts and Hole It 3D have no clock pushing you, and you can put the phone down mid-level. For a longer run with real decisions, pick Meteorfall: Krumit's Tale or Cards of Terra. For quick arcade reflexes, POINPY and FallDown! III.

If you play on a big iPhone, turn on Reachability in Settings, Accessibility, Touch. A swipe down on the bottom edge then brings the top of the screen within reach of your thumb, which helps with menus at the top.

Questions about one handed games

What games can you play with one hand on iPhone?

Brick breakers like One More Brick and holedown, tap puzzles like Hexascapes and Nuts And Bolts, card games like Meteorfall: Krumit's Tale and Cards of Terra, and arcade games like POINPY and FallDown! III. All nine here promise one-handed or one-finger play on their App Store page.

Are there one-handed games that aren't puzzles?

Yes. Meteorfall: Krumit's Tale is a roguelike deckbuilder played one-handed in portrait mode, POINPY is a vertical climber, and FallDown! III is an endless arcade game with a Zen mode.

Which one-handed games work offline?

One More Brick, Meteorfall: Krumit's Tale and Cards of Terra say in their App Store descriptions that they work offline. Check the store page for the others.

Are these one-handed games free?

Seven of the nine are free to download. holedown costs $3.99 and Meteorfall: Krumit's Tale costs $6.99 in the US. Cards of Terra shows ads that one purchase removes.

How can I make my iPhone easier to use with one hand?

Turn on Reachability in Settings, Accessibility, Touch. Then swipe down on the bottom edge of the screen to pull the top half within reach of your thumb.
