Games like Ultimate Fishing Simulator: 10 fishing sims for iPhone in 2026

Ultimate Fishing Simulator from Ultimate Games is a realistic fishing sim best known on PC and consoles. Its iPhone version is still in the App Store, but it hasn't been updated since December 2017 and is English only. If you want that kind of fishing on your phone today, these ten games are maintained alternatives: Fishing Planet for the full sim, Carpcraft for carp, Ice Fishing Derby 2 and Pro Pilkki 2 for ice, Fly Fishing Simulator HD for fly fishing, and a calm lake on your Apple Watch.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest, and know that it is a cozy game, not a simulator. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one is built around tackle choices, real waters or fighting the fish, plays on iPhone and was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026.

    Cozy, real-time · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.8 (16)

    Not a simulator, but a lake that runs on your real clock: dawn, day, dusk and night, and each of the 20 species bites at its own hours. You upgrade the line from 10 to 200 m and baits from bread to glowworm. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and on Apple Watch you reel with the Digital Crown, no iPhone needed.

  2. 2. Fishing Planet

    Full fishing simulator · Free · ★ 4.8 (21.9K)

    The closest thing to Ultimate Fishing Simulator on iPhone. More than 200 species whose behavior depends on season, weather, time of day and water temperature, 26 waterways based on real places, and float, spinning, bottom and trolling rigs. It's an online multiplayer sim, free to download.

  3. 3. Carpcraft: Carp Fishing

    Carp fishing sim · Free · ★ 4.5 (48)

    A real-time carp fishing game with a full day-night cycle: you fish with up to four rods on float or ledger and wait for the run, even at 2 am. Five lakes and a canal, 16 freshwater species and 45 target fish. You upgrade rods, reels, line, hooks and bait, and bite notifications tell you when something takes. Only in English.

  4. 4. Ice Fishing Derby 2

    Ice fishing sim · Free · ★ 4.8 (1.2K)

    A 3D ice fishing game where you drill holes, set up a shanty, jig lures and read the water with sonar or an underwater camera. Multi-day derbies make you manage money, gear and body heat, or you can fish freely. Free to play; a Lake Pass opens all 22 lakes. Only in English.

  5. 5. Fly Fishing Simulator HD

    Fly fishing sim · Free · ★ 4.4 (383)

    A first-person fly fishing simulator built on photos of real rivers. You control the rod and line directly, tie your own flies in 3D and choose from more than 160 patterns. The free download includes a practice pond and six sites on one trout stream; more than 200 rivers are paid. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Pro Pilkki 2 Ice Fishing Game

    Ice fishing multiplayer · Free · ★ 4.4 (30)

    The mobile version of a long-running ice fishing simulation with more than 40 frozen lakes, ponds and rivers. The free trial gives you 16 lakes, practice and single-player competitions; the full version adds real-time online games, 25 competition modes, three seasons and four times of day. Only in English.

  7. 7. Fish Rain: fishing simulator

    Float fishing sim · Free · ★ 4.7 (564)

    A float fishing simulator with more than 200 species and waters from South America to Lake Baikal. You match the rod to the weight of the fish, and the rod breaks if you overdo it. Its App Store page says you can play without internet. Only in English.

  8. 8. uCaptain Fishing Boat Game

    Boat and commercial fishing · Free · ★ 4.7 (13.5K)

    You build and fit out your own boat with nets, crab pots and lobster traps, set the drag on your reel and pick lures for more than 60 species. The open map runs from Venice, Louisiana, to Dutch Harbor, Alaska, and the Bering Sea. Only in English.

    Multiplayer sport fishing · Free · ★ 4.8 (159K)

    Nearly 160,000 ratings in the App Store. Spots from the Florida coast and the Kenai River to Lake Biwa and Loch Ness. You keep the line tension in the center while the fish fights, then duel other players and join championships.

  10. 10. Fishing Tour

    Sport fishing tournaments · Free · ★ 4.8 (1.1K)

    From SOZAP: spots from the Caribbean Sea and Swedish lakes to Florida, with rods, bait and tackle to customize and upgrade. Real-time tournaments against other players replace the slow solo trips of Ultimate Fishing Simulator.

Is Ultimate Fishing Simulator on iPhone?

Yes, but it's old. Ultimate Fishing Simulator is free in the US App Store from Ultimate Games, rated 4.2 from 641 ratings. Its last update came out in December 2017, and it's only in English. Its page lists 12 real fishing locations, a choice of tackle and tournaments. For a sim that still gets updates, start with Fishing Planet.

Which alternative fits which kind of angler?

Want the whole sim with many waters: Fishing Planet. Carp and night bites: Carpcraft. Ice: Ice Fishing Derby 2 for tactics and money management, Pro Pilkki 2 for online competitions. Fly fishing: Fly Fishing Simulator HD. Boats: uCaptain.

Fishing Clash and Fishing Tour trade some realism for short sessions against other players. Fish & Chill isn't a simulator at all: it's a calm lake on real time, for a few casts on your wrist.
