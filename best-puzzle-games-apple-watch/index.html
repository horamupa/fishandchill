The best puzzle games for Apple Watch in 2026

A small screen suits puzzles better than almost anything else: one grid, a few taps, and you can stop whenever the bus arrives. These ten puzzle games for Apple Watch cover sudoku, solitaire, 2048, word games, sliding puzzles, mahjong and quick brain training.

We make Fish & Chill, a cozy fishing game for Apple Watch. It isn't a puzzle game, so it's not on this list. All ten games below come from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one is built around puzzles, shows an Apple Watch version with watch screenshots on its App Store page, holds a rating of 4.3 or higher and was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026.

  1. 1. Wordfully

    Word puzzle · Free · ★ 4.7 (11K)

    Guess a short word from colored letter hints. Play as many rounds as you like from a curated list of more than 15,000 words, or raise the stakes with 6- and 7-letter words and a mode that gives you 10 seconds per guess.

    View on the App Store

  2. 2. Solitaire The Game

    Cards · Free · ★ 4.7 (4.3K)

    Klondike with a proper watch version: 1-card draw, standard scoring and a layout for your left or right hand. Undo, autocomplete and statistics are there too, so a game on your wrist feels complete.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. 2048+#

    Number puzzle · Free · ★ 4.7 (2.6K)

    Merge tiles past 2048, then keep going. The app steps up from the classic 4×4 board to 12×12, and larger boards add several new tiles per move. An Apple Watch version comes with it.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Sudoku Puzzle – Watch & Phone

    Sudoku · Free · ★ 4.7 (690)

    The pick for sudoku on Apple Watch. Choose a quick 6×6 grid, a standard 9×9 or a jigsaw board, and come back for the daily challenge.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. Car Park Puzzle Watch & Phone

    Sliding puzzle · Free · ★ 4.5 (2.4K)

    Shift the parked cars until the way out is clear. Over 1,000 handcrafted levels, and each has exactly one solution, so every board is a small logic problem.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Snappy Word Search

    Word search · Free · ★ 4.7 (2K)

    Trace across the letters to spell the hidden words. There are 400 levels plus a four-letter mode, and extra words you find pay out gems for hints. Watch players get their own ranking.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. Quarti: Word Puzzles

    Word puzzle · Free · ★ 4.8 (913)

    An ad-free word game with two moods: Zen Mode gives unlimited hints, one word at a time, while Lightning Mode times you over 10 words. Free daily hints and color themes round it out.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. Block Puzzle: blast the square

    Block puzzle · Free · ★ 4.6 (806)

    Puzzles with a light story: help a raccoon with the harvest, then move from the kitchen to the jungle and beyond. Wins unlock new puzzle machines that play differently.

    View on the App Store

  9. 9. Mahjong: match puzzle

    Mahjong · Free · ★ 4.7 (69)

    Match pairs of free tiles while a small mystery about a girl in the rain unfolds. Lo-fi music and rain sounds keep it relaxed, with zero pressure, and it plays on the watch as well.

    View on the App Store

  10. 10. Brainess – Train your Brain

    Brain training · Paid · ★ 4.3 (199)

    Seven short exercises for memory, attention and mental math, from matching card pairs to choosing the right sign in an equation. It tracks a playful brain age. The last update dates from 2021, but it's still on sale.

    View on the App Store

Questions about Apple Watch puzzle games

What puzzle games can you play on Apple Watch?

Sudoku, Klondike solitaire, 2048, word games, sliding car puzzles, block puzzles, mahjong and brain training all have watch versions. The ten games above were checked in the App Store in November 2026.

Is there sudoku on Apple Watch?

Yes. Sudoku Puzzle – Watch & Phone has 6×6, 9×9 and jigsaw grids plus a daily challenge, and it's free to download.

Can you play a Wordle-style game on Apple Watch?

Yes. Wordfully uses the same idea of guessing a word from colored letters, and it has a watch version. It's a separate game from an independent developer, not the official Wordle.

Are Apple Watch puzzle games free?

Nine of the ten games here are free to download. Brainess costs $0.99 in the US. Some free games sell optional in-app purchases, and Quarti is the only one that promises no ads in its App Store description.

Which watchOS do these games need?

It varies, so check each App Store page. Wordfully needs watchOS 10 or later, while Snappy Word Search and Brainess run on watchOS 4 or later.
