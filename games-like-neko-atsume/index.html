Games like Neko Atsume: 10 cozy collecting games for iPhone in 2026

In Neko Atsume you put out food and toys, leave, and come back to see which cats turned up. The closest games like Neko Atsume on iPhone are Purrfect Spirits, with cats and a ghost for a host, and Usagi Shima, with bunnies on an island. The other eight keep the short visits and the collection, and trade the yard for a kitchen, a village or a lake.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest, and know that it's about fish, not cats, and you catch them yourself. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one is calm, built around collecting, free to download and was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026.

  1. 1. Fish & Chill

    Fish instead of cats · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.8 (18)

    Who shows up depends on when you look, and here it's the real hour. The lake follows your clock through dawn, day, dusk and night, and each fish has its own biting hours, so the evening visitors won't come at noon. Unlike in Neko Atsume you do the catching: cast, reel, upgrade the line from 10 to 200 m. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and the whole game also runs on Apple Watch.

    Download on the App Store

  2. 2. Purrfect Spirits

    Closest match · Free · ★ 4.8 (15K)

    You've become a ghost and have no idea how to move on, so you look after the cats that drop by your house each day. Feed them, buy scratchers and towers, redecorate, and use the right items to draw out Rare Cats. It adds what Neko Atsume never had: a story, told in short manga-style scenes. Saves can't be moved to another device.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. Usagi Shima

    Bunnies on an island · Free · ★ 4.9 (2.7K)

    Swap the yard for an abandoned island and the cats for rabbits. You place toys, plants and buildings to attract over 30 bunnies, and rare ones come only under the right conditions. The island matches your time of day, the shop refreshes four times daily, and a BunBook keeps track of everyone you've met. You can also pet, brush and play hide and seek with them.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Tsuki's Odyssey

    Passive village life · Free · ★ 4.5 (9.5K)

    Tsuki is a rabbit who lives in Mushroom Village and does as they please. You decorate the home, make friends, catch fish, and check in to see what's going on. If your favorite part of Neko Atsume was opening the app just to peek, this is that feeling with a whole village. The store page warns that some content isn't for children.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. Adorable Home

    Home and cats · Free · ★ 4.7 (123K)

    You and your partner have moved into a nearly empty house with a cat named Snow. Cook, feed the cats, earn love, and spend it on furniture and more cats. The more of them you have, the more moments you can photograph for the album. The developer suggests checking in every couple of hours. It's a game about a couple, with some revealing outfits, so not one for kids.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Cats & Soup

    Cats that cook · Free · ★ 4.9 (340K)

    An illustrated forest where cats chop, stir and boil soup whether you're watching or not. You collect breeds from Ragdoll to Maine Coon, name them, dress them up, take photos, and feed them fish from a mini-game. There's more to upgrade here than in Neko Atsume, so it suits people who want numbers going up along with the cats.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. Cats are Cute

    Cat village · Free · ★ 4.9 (29K)

    A quiet town that grows with each cat you collect, every one with its own face and small habits. You upgrade buildings, unlock corners and place decorations, and rewards build up while the app is closed. The store page promises no missions and no timers, which makes it one of the least demanding picks here.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. Mochicats Collection

    Squishy cats · Free · ★ 4.9 (6.7K)

    More than 50 round, mochi-like cats who want dessert. Feed them and they invite themselves in, then you can poke them, pat them and stack them into a pile that gets taller as the collection grows. An optional monthly subscription adds automatic poking and collecting.

    View on the App Store

  9. 9. KleptoCats

    Cats who steal · Free · ★ 4.8 (32K)

    Neko Atsume cats leave you fish as thanks. These ones rob people. You send a cat out, it returns with something odd, and your bare room slowly fills with the loot. There are hundreds of cats to collect, plus clues to the mystery of how they ended up with you. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  10. 10. Forest Island

    Animals and nature · Free · ★ 4.8 (26K)

    For anyone who wants more than cats: over 100 animals and birds, among them rabbits, ducks and raccoons. You clear debris from the sea, gather hearts, and add forests, lakes and meadows to your island. A relaxation mode plays calm music with ocean, rain and bird sounds.

    View on the App Store

Which game is closest to Neko Atsume?

Purrfect Spirits is the closest: cats visit, you feed them, and special items bring the rare ones. Usagi Shima is next, with the same idea of decorating a place and waiting to see who comes, only with bunnies. If you just want more of the original, Neko Atsume 2 is out and still gets updates.

For watching without doing much, pick Tsuki's Odyssey or Cats are Cute. For decorating, Adorable Home. For steady upgrades, Cats & Soup or Forest Island. If you'd like a collection you catch with your own hands, and without ads, try Fish & Chill.

Questions about games like Neko Atsume

What games are like Neko Atsume?

On iPhone: Purrfect Spirits for visiting cats, Usagi Shima for bunnies, Tsuki's Odyssey for a village you check in on, Adorable Home for a house with cats, Cats & Soup and Cats are Cute for idle cat collecting. Fish & Chill has the same short visits and collection, with fish.

What is the difference between Neko Atsume and Neko Atsume 2?

The basics are the same: put out toys and snacks, wait for cats, log them in the Catbook. Neko Atsume 2 adds visits to other players' yards and Helper cats, which come with a paid subscription called Cat's Club Support. Some features need an internet connection.

Can you still get the original Neko Atsume?

Yes. In November 2026 Neko Atsume: Kitty Collector is still free in the US App Store, but its last update was in March 2024. Apple Arcade subscribers also have Neko Atsume: Kitty Collector+. Neko Atsume 2 was updated in November 2026.

Is there a game like Neko Atsume without ads?

Fish & Chill has no ads: it's free, with one optional purchase. It's fishing, not cat collecting. With Apple Arcade you can play Neko Atsume: Kitty Collector+, since Arcade games don't carry ads. The App Store page of Neko Atsume 2 says it contains advertising.

What games are like Cats & Soup?

Cats are Cute is the nearest on this list: an idle cat town that earns rewards while the app is closed. Forest Island has the same steady growth with over 100 animals. Mochicats Collection is simpler, about feeding and stacking cats.
