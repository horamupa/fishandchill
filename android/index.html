The same game on your wrist

Cozy Fishing is drawn natively for Wear OS. Tap to cast, turn the rotating crown to reel and feel the bite and the fight through haptics. A session fits a bus stop or a queue.

CAST

Stop the bait at any depth

Tap to cast and watch the bait sink. Where you stop it is your bet. Shallow water bites fast but small, and the deep holds fish that snap a starter line. Fish see the bait, circle it and tease you with false nibbles before they take it.

FIGHT

Turn the crown, feel the fight

Keep the line tension in the sweet zone. Reel too hard and it snaps, give too much slack and the hook falls out. On Wear OS you turn the rotating crown and read every run and head shake through vibration. On the phone a swipe does the same job with one hand.

LAKE

A lake that keeps changing

Dawn, noon, sunset and night roll into each other with every cast. Rain, storms and fog change who bites. Clear nights bring stars and, once in a while, the northern lights. On the phone you hear it too: water, weather, the reel and the bite.

COLLECT

30 species and a journal that fills slowly

Uncaught species swim as dark shadows. Your first catch paints the fish in and opens its page in the journal. Every fish is a live silhouette, so you can read its species and size before it bites and hunt the one you want.

DIVE

Ten rods, down to 200 m

You start with a driftwood branch at 5 m and finish in the abyss at 200 m. Deep species glow in the dark. Below 22 m lives the Leviathan, a 140 kg catfish that breaks any gear you haven't prepared. Bread, worm, bloodworm, spoon, glowworm and live bait each draw different fish.

Questions

Is Cozy Fishing free?

Yes, down to 10 m. One optional one-time purchase unlocks the deeper rods forever. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It needs no internet and no account, on the phone and on the watch.

Does it run on Galaxy Watch or Pixel Watch?

Cozy Fishing runs on Wear OS, and Pixel Watch and Galaxy Watch 4 and later use Wear OS. Google Play shows whether your model is compatible before you install.

How long does a session take?

A full fishing session takes 15 to 40 seconds, from the cast to the catch.

Is this Fish & Chill for Android?

No. Cozy Fishing is a separate game from the same team, with its own art, 30 species and its own progression. Fish & Chill is on Apple Watch and iPhone.
