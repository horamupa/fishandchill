Games like A Little to the Left: 10 tidy puzzle games for iPhone in 2026

A Little to the Left is about putting household things in just the right order while a cat messes them up. The closest games like A Little to the Left on iPhone are its own two expansions, Cupboards & Drawers and Seeing Stars, sold as separate apps. After those, Unpacking, Empty. and I Love Hue keep the same quiet pleasure of making order out of a mess.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. It isn't a tidy puzzle: it's a fishing game, and we included it for people who like a calm collection to complete. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one is about arranging, sorting or restoring things at your own pace and was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026.

  1. 1. Fish & Chill

    A collection instead of a shelf · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.8 (20)

    If the best part of tidying for you is the feeling of a complete set, this is that feeling in a fishing game. 20 species live in a lake that follows your real clock, and each one bites at its own hours, so filling the collection takes days of short visits. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and the whole game also runs on Apple Watch.

    Download on the App Store

  2. 2. A Little to the Left: Drawers

    Closest match · Paid · ★ 4.9 (1.7K)

    The first expansion from the same studio: 25 new puzzles set in cupboards and drawers, mixed with classic sorting levels. Some puzzles sit inside other puzzles, some levels take several steps, and you move objects between drawers you open and close. There are secret compartments and four new breaks to pet the cat. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. A Little to the Left: Stars

    More solutions · Paid · ★ 4.9 (850)

    The second expansion is built around finding more than one answer. 33 new levels plus 5 bonus ones, up to five solutions per level and 100 stars to collect. New items can be folded, crushed, stuck together, bounced and strummed. There's a new soundtrack and more than one cat. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Unpacking

    Unpacking a life · Paid · ★ 4.8 (6.8K)

    You take things out of boxes and fit them into a new home, over eight moves from a single bedroom to a whole house. There are no timers, meters or scores. You never meet the owner, but what comes along to each home, and what gets left behind, tells the story. It won a BAFTA for narrative.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. Empty.

    Less is more · Free · ★ 4.8 (2.6K)

    Each level is a room you clear by rotating it, with one touch and one hand. More than 25 hand-made levels and a full soundtrack album. The developer promises no ads and no paid content: the game is supported by donations. A short one, for an evening. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. I Love Hue

    Order out of color · Free · ★ 4.9 (50K)

    Instead of objects you arrange colored tiles into perfect gradients. It's a puzzle of perception, not logic: you learn to see the smallest difference between two shades. More than 900 levels and a soft synth soundtrack, so there's plenty to sort.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. Assemble with Care

    Restoring things · Free · ★ 3.6 (1.4K)

    From the studio behind Monument Valley. Maria, an antique restorer, arrives in a small town and takes broken objects apart to put them back together, and with them the lives of their owners. The first chapters are free, one purchase opens the rest, and there are no ads. Its rating is lower than the others on this list.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. Art of Fauna: Cozy Puzzles

    Vintage puzzles · Free · ★ 4.9 (5.9K)

    Jigsaw puzzles made from 18th and 19th century natural history drawings, each paired with a short text about the animal. You can piece together the picture or rebuild the text. It won an Apple Design Award for inclusivity in 2025, and part of every purchase goes to wildlife conservation.

    View on the App Store

  9. 9. Satiszone: Perfect ASMR Tidy

    Quick tidy-ups · Free · ★ 4.8 (50K)

    Dozens of small organizing puzzles: you tap, drag, slide and draw to put things in order, and every move answers with calming sounds and vibration. Less clever than A Little to the Left, but good for a minute or two at a time.

    View on the App Store

  10. 10. Perfect Tidy

    The most popular · Free · ★ 4.6 (369K)

    The best-known tidy game in the App Store by number of ratings. Mini-games about sorting, tidying and playing with cute things, with ASMR sounds and haptics. The age rating lists loot boxes, so it's built more like a typical free mobile game.

    View on the App Store

Which game is closest to A Little to the Left?

Its own expansions are the closest: A Little to the Left: Drawers and A Little to the Left: Stars, each a separate paid app from the same studio. The original is free to try with 9 puzzles, and one purchase opens over 100 more. After that, Unpacking is the best next game: the same calm placing of things, plus a story.

For something short and free without ads, pick Empty. For sorting by color, I Love Hue. For taking objects apart and fixing them, Assemble with Care. For quick satisfying minutes, Satiszone or Perfect Tidy. If you like order in the form of a collection you fill day by day, try Fish & Chill.

Questions about games like A Little to the Left

What games are like A Little to the Left?

On iPhone: A Little to the Left: Drawers and A Little to the Left: Stars, Unpacking, Empty., I Love Hue, Assemble with Care and Art of Fauna. Satiszone and Perfect Tidy are simpler free tidy games. Fish & Chill is a calm fishing game for those who like completing a collection.

Is A Little to the Left free on iPhone?

It's free to try. In November 2026 the free version has 9 puzzles from the main game, 3 Daily Tidy puzzles and 1 level from the Archive. A one-time purchase opens over 100 puzzles, the full Daily Tidy Delivery and the seasonal Archive. There are no ads.

Are there more A Little to the Left games?

Yes, two expansions, each sold as its own app in the App Store: Cupboards & Drawers with 25 new puzzles, and Seeing Stars with 33 new levels, 5 bonus levels and up to five solutions per level. All three games are in English only.

Is Unpacking like A Little to the Left?

Close. Both are about putting things in the right place, without timers or scores. A Little to the Left is a set of separate logic puzzles, while Unpacking is one story told through eight house moves. Unpacking is a paid app and is available in 15 languages.

Are there tidy games without ads?

Yes. The developers of A Little to the Left, Empty. and Assemble with Care say there are no ads. Fish & Chill has no ads either: it's free, with one optional purchase.
