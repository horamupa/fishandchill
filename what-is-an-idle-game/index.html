What is an idle game? Idle vs active fishing games explained

An idle game is a game that keeps making progress with little or no input from you. You tap a little at the start, spend what you earn on upgrades that work automatically, and come back later to collect. Idle fishing games apply this loop to boats, crews and catches. Active fishing games do the opposite: every fish is caught by hand.

We make Fish & Chill, an active fishing game for Apple Watch and iPhone. It is not an idle game, and we say so below. We describe idle games as fairly as we can, because they suit plenty of players.

What is an idle game?

An idle game, also called an incremental or clicker game, is built around numbers that keep growing. You start with simple taps to earn a little currency, then buy upgrades, workers or machines that earn for you. Progress keeps ticking while you do something else, and many idle games add up what you earned while the app was closed.

Most idle games also have a reset, often called prestige: you start over in exchange for a permanent bonus that makes the next run faster. The appeal is steady growth with low effort. You check in for a minute, make a few choices, and leave.

How idle fishing games work

An idle fishing game wraps that loop in a fishing theme. Fish turn into money, money buys better boats, rods and crew, and the crew keeps fishing for you. The skill is in choosing upgrades, not in landing any single fish.

Hooked Inc is the best-known example on iPhone. Its App Store page calls it an idle clicker: you swipe to catch fish and earn money, hire crew members, upgrade your boat and unlock new regions. It is free and holds 4.7 stars from more than 500,000 ratings in the US App Store.

What an active fishing game asks of you

In an active fishing game nothing happens until you act. You cast, wait for a bite, set the hook and fight the fish yourself, and the catch depends on how you play that moment. Put the game down and the lake waits for you.

Fish & Chill works this way. You cast with a tap, then reel in the line: on Apple Watch by turning the Digital Crown, with haptic ticks for every meter you win back, on iPhone by dragging the ReelPad while you watch the tension. Keep the line in the red zone too long and it snaps.

Idle vs active fishing games: the key differences

The main difference is who does the work. In an idle game the upgrades fish for you, so progress comes from time and smart spending. In an active game you fish, so progress comes from what you catch and learn.

That changes how each one feels. Idle games reward short check-ins and long breaks, and a missed day costs little. Active games ask for your full attention for a few minutes and give a clear win or loss each time. Idle fishing games are often about growing an empire, active ones about one fish on the line.

Which kind should you play?

Pick an idle fishing game if you like watching numbers climb, want progress while your phone sits in your pocket, and enjoy planning upgrades more than reflexes.

Pick an active fishing game if you want the catch itself to be the fun: the bite, the pull, the moment the line goes tight. Active games also suit places where you can give a few minutes of full attention, like a train ride or a coffee break.

Questions about idle games

What does idle mean in gaming?

Idle means the game keeps progressing while you are not playing. Upgrades, workers or machines earn for you, and you return to collect and spend.

Is an idle game the same as a clicker game?

Mostly. Clicker games start with tapping or clicking for currency, then add automatic income, which makes them idle games. Both are also called incremental games.

What is an idle fishing game?

A fishing-themed idle game: fish earn money, money buys boats, rods and crew, and the crew keeps fishing for you. Hooked Inc is a well-known example on iPhone.

Is Fish & Chill an idle game?

No. Fish & Chill is an active fishing game: you cast, reel and fight every fish yourself. It has no energy timers or ads, and fish bite at their own real-world hours.
