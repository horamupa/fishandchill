What makes an Android game cozy

A cozy game asks for little and punishes even less. There's no timer counting you down, no rival stealing your spot, and nothing breaks if you put the phone away for a day. Most cozy games repeat a small, pleasant action: casting, planting, tidying, decorating.

On Android, check the labels under the title on Google Play. "Contains ads" and "In-app purchases" are listed separately. A calm game can still nag you with video ads, so if that breaks the mood, start with Cozy Fishing, Stardew Valley, Sky, A Little to the Left, Townscaper or Florence.
