Games like Animal Crossing: 10 picks for iPhone in 2026

Looking for games like Animal Crossing on your phone? The closest on iPhone is Nintendo's own Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete, a one-time purchase with no extra in-app spending. For a free open town with fishing, gardening and friends, try Heartopia. Forest Fables brings the animal villagers, and Fish & Chill keeps just the fishing, on a lake that follows your real clock.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one shares part of the Animal Crossing loop: friendly neighbors, a home or town to decorate, fishing or collecting, and a slow daily rhythm. All are rated 4.5 or higher and were on sale in the US App Store in November 2026. Apple Arcade and Netflix games are left out because they need a subscription.

  1. 1. Fish & Chill

    Calm fishing · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.8 (18)

    For players who spent most of their island days with a fishing rod. Like Animal Crossing, the lake runs on your real clock: dawn, day, dusk and night, and each of the 20 species bites at its own hours. Upgrade the line from 10 to 200 m and baits from bread to glowworm. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and it runs fully on Apple Watch, where you reel with the Digital Crown.

    Download on the App Store

  2. 2. Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete

    The real Animal Crossing · Paid · ★ 4.8 (9.9K)

    Nintendo's one-time purchase version of Pocket Camp, packed with seven years of items and events and no extra in-game purchases. You manage a campsite, fish, catch bugs, chat with animals that have quirky personalities and decorate with more than 10,000 items. It costs $19.99 once, and it is the only Animal Crossing game on iPhone.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. Heartopia

    Open cozy town · Free · ★ 4.6 (36K)

    A free life sim with a lot of freedom from XD. Water the flowers, go fishing, cook, garden, birdwatch and play music, then build your dream house, alone or together with friends you invite over. You can adopt cats and dogs and choose from more than 1,000 outfits. You meet new people every time you log in, so it feels more like a social world than a private island.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Forest Fables: Cozy pixel sim

    Animal villagers · Free · ★ 4.8 (1.7K)

    A pixel-art life sim set in Wildleaf Forest, with 23 animal villagers who each have a story and a quest for you. You help them, earn friendship stamps, unlock new areas and decorate your woodland home. Minigames cover baking, coffee, carrot farming and ice cream. The App Store page says it has ads and optional in-app purchases.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. Tsuki's Odyssey

    Mushroom Village · Free · ★ 4.5 (9.5K)

    A passive adventure: Tsuki lives in Mushroom Village with a cast of odd neighbors. You decorate the home, make friends and catch all kinds of fish, but Tsuki is not your pet and moves around on their own. Check in now and then to see what happened in town. The App Store rates it 17+.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Cats&Soup: Relaxing Cozy Games

    Cat soup forest · Free · ★ 4.9 (340K)

    An idle game set in a peaceful animal forest where cats cook soup. Each cat breed looks distinct, you dress them in hats and outfits, take photos and feed them fish from a fishing minigame. ASMR kitchen sounds and your own BGM playlist keep it calm. With about 340,000 ratings, it's one of the most loved cozy games on the App Store.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. Harvest Town - Pixel Sim RPG

    Village life RPG · Free · ★ 4.8 (19K)

    A pixel farm RPG for anyone who liked the village side of Animal Crossing. You fix up a cottage, raise chickens, ducks and cattle, adopt a cat or dog and get to know villagers with their own stories, up to marrying one of them. Four seasons change the town. It also has online features like market trading and races with other players.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. Pocket Love!

    Tiny home and couple · Free · ★ 4.7 (250K)

    From HyperBeard: create a couple, dress them up, adopt a pet and build out their tiny home room by room, from kitchen and garden to a movie theater or even a fossil museum. If decorating your Animal Crossing house was the best part, this one is all about that, with new furniture and clothes to unbox.

    View on the App Store

  9. 9. Animal Restaurant

    Animal restaurant · Free · ★ 4.9 (168K)

    It starts with a stray cat in the forest who becomes your first employee. You cook dishes like taiyaki and pancakes, decorate the restaurant in many styles, hire cat staff and get to know animal customers through chats and letters, sometimes changing how their stories go. Rated 4.9 from about 168,000 ratings.

    View on the App Store

  10. 10. Campfire Cat Cafe

    Cat cafe in the forest · Free · ★ 4.8 (15K)

    An idle cooking game: open a cafe in an animal forest, hire cat staff and serve animal customers who tell funny stories. Your cats earn tips while you're away. You travel from a food truck to a fancy cafe, a seaside bakery and a snowy mountain camp with a hot spring, and decorate each place your way.

    View on the App Store

Pick by what you love in Animal Crossing

If you want Animal Crossing itself, buy Pocket Camp Complete: it's the only official one on iPhone and has no extra purchases. If you love the neighbors, Forest Fables and Tsuki's Odyssey have the most character. If it's decorating, try Pocket Love! or Heartopia. If it's running a little shop in a town of animals, Animal Restaurant, Cats&Soup or Campfire Cat Cafe.

And if your favorite hours on the island were spent fishing as the sun went down, Fish & Chill is fishing and nothing else, on a lake whose day and night follow your real clock, just like Animal Crossing.

Ratings: US App Store, November 2026.

Questions about games like Animal Crossing

What games are like Animal Crossing on iPhone?

Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete is the official one. Heartopia is a free open cozy town, Forest Fables and Tsuki's Odyssey have animal neighbors, Pocket Love! is about decorating, Cats&Soup and Animal Restaurant are cozy animal shops, and Fish & Chill is calm real-time fishing.

Is Animal Crossing on iPhone?

Yes, as Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp Complete. It's a $19.99 one-time purchase in the US App Store with no extra in-game purchases, rated 4.8 in November 2026. Animal Crossing: New Horizons is only on Nintendo Switch.

Are there free games like Animal Crossing?

Yes. Heartopia, Forest Fables, Tsuki's Odyssey, Cats&Soup, Harvest Town, Pocket Love!, Animal Restaurant, Campfire Cat Cafe and Fish & Chill are free to download. Most have optional in-app purchases.

Which game like Animal Crossing has fishing?

Pocket Camp Complete, Heartopia, Tsuki's Odyssey and Harvest Town all have fishing, and Cats&Soup has a fishing minigame. Fish & Chill is built entirely around it: 20 species, each biting at its own real-time hours.

Is there an Animal Crossing style game for Apple Watch?

Not a full town sim. The closest in spirit is Fish & Chill: a cozy fishing game that runs fully on Apple Watch, with a lake where day and night follow your real clock.
