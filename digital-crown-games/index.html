Digital Crown games: Apple Watch games you play with the crown

The Digital Crown is the best game controller on Apple Watch: you turn it without covering the screen, and it gives precise, one-handed control. These nine games use the crown as their main control on the watch, from reeling in a fish to steering a race car, aiming a cue or moving a paddle.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest. The other eight games come from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one has an Apple Watch version, says in its App Store description that you play it on the watch with the Digital Crown, and was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026. Why the Digital Crown works as a game controller

Touch controls on a small watch screen hide the game under your finger. The Digital Crown sits on the side, so the whole screen stays visible, and it reads small turns precisely. That's why paddle games, shooters and racers on Apple Watch steer with it, while a tap on the screen usually means fire, shoot or jump.

The crown also suits slow, physical actions. In Fish & Chill you wind the line in by turning it, and a haptic tick marks each meter you win back, so reeling feels close to a real fishing reel.

How to tell if a watch game uses the crown

Read the App Store description before you buy: games built around the crown say so plainly, usually in a line about controls. Check the Apple Watch section of the page too, since some games are iPhone apps whose watch part only sends notifications. Every game on this list plays on the watch itself.

Questions about Digital Crown games

What Apple Watch games use the Digital Crown?

Fish & Chill uses it to reel in fish, Ping Pong and MiniGames to move a paddle, Kepler Attack and Jupiter Attack to fly a ship, Touch Round to steer a car, and Air Hockey Wear and Billiard Wear to move and aim. All nine games above were checked in November 2026.

Is there a fishing game for Apple Watch that uses the crown?

Yes. In Fish & Chill you turn the Digital Crown to reel in, with a haptic tick for every meter of line. It's free, has no ads, works offline and runs fully on the watch.

Are Digital Crown games free?

Five of the nine are free to download: Fish & Chill, Ping Pong, Touch Round, Billiard Wear and The Wrist Rush. Kepler Attack, MiniGames, Jupiter Attack and Air Hockey Wear are paid apps, $1.99 each in the US.

Can you play these games without an iPhone nearby?

Fish & Chill and Air Hockey Wear say in their descriptions that they play on the watch with no iPhone needed. The others run on the watch too, but some, like Touch Round and Billiard Wear, need the iPhone app to send a watch score to Game Center.
