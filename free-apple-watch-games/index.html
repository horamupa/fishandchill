What "free" means for Apple Watch games

Every game on this list costs nothing to download, but free rarely means the whole story. Some free games show ads or sell extras, such as hints, skins or ad removal. Others are free to install and then ask for a subscription, which is why subscription-only game collections are left out here.

Before you install, scroll to "In-App Purchases" on the App Store page: it lists what the game sells and at what price. Fish & Chill has no ads and no energy timers; the only purchase is an optional one-time PRO.

Which free game to pick

For a calm minute: Fish & Chill, Solitaire The Game or Sudoku Puzzle. For words: Wordfully or Snappy Word Search. For a quick logic fix: 2048+# or Car Park Puzzle. For reflexes: Dare the Monkey, Hit The Island or Ping Pong.

Questions about free Apple Watch games

Are there free games for Apple Watch?

Yes. All ten games on this list are free to download and play on the watch: Fish & Chill, Dare the Monkey, Hit The Island, Wordfully, Ping Pong, Solitaire The Game, 2048+#, Car Park Puzzle, Snappy Word Search and Sudoku Puzzle.

How do you download free games on Apple Watch?

Open the App Store on the watch, search for the game and tap Get. Or install it on iPhone, open the Watch app, go to My Watch, find the game under Available Apps and tap Install.

Do free Apple Watch games have ads or in-app purchases?

Some do. The App Store page lists in-app purchases for each game, so check it before you install. Fish & Chill has no ads; it offers one optional one-time PRO purchase.

Can you play free Apple Watch games without an iPhone?

Once installed, the games on this list run on the watch. Some sync progress through their iPhone app when it's nearby. Fish & Chill plays fully on the watch and works offline.
