Can you play games on Galaxy Watch? Yes, and here's how to install them

Yes, you can play games on a Galaxy Watch. Galaxy Watch4 and every newer model run Wear OS, so they install games from Google Play like any other watch app, and so does Pixel Watch. Games play right on the wrist with taps, swipes and the bezel or crown. Below: how to install games on Galaxy Watch, from the watch or from your phone, and what to expect.

We make Cozy Fishing, a fishing game for Android phones and Wear OS watches, and use it as the example in a few places. The install steps work the same for any watch game.

Which watches can play Wear OS games?

Any watch running Wear OS can install games from Google Play. That covers Galaxy Watch4 and all newer Galaxy Watch models, Pixel Watch, and watches from other brands that run Wear OS. Older Galaxy watches such as Galaxy Watch3 and Watch Active2 run Tizen, so Google Play games won't install on them.

Before you install, check the game's Google Play page. Games with a watch version can be installed on your watch from the phone, and the description usually says whether the game itself runs on the watch or only sends notifications there.

How to install games from the watch

Open the Play Store app on the watch and search by voice or keyboard, or scroll through the featured apps. Open the game and tap Install. When the download finishes, the game appears in the watch's app list.

The watch needs a connection to download: Wi-Fi, the paired phone, or LTE on cellular models. The watch's Play Store also lists watch versions of apps you already have on your phone, so a game you installed there may be one tap away.

How to install apps on Galaxy Watch from your phone

Open the Play Store on your phone and find the game. If it has a watch version, the Install button lets you pick your watch as the install target. Choose the watch and install, and the game is sent to it over the connection.

On a Galaxy Watch, the Galaxy Wearable app on the phone also shows the apps installed on the watch, and on a Pixel Watch the Pixel Watch app does the same. Use them to check that a game actually reached the watch.

How you play: touch, bezel, crown and haptics

Most watch games are built around one or two simple inputs. You tap or swipe the screen, and on many models you also turn something: the rotating bezel on Galaxy Watch Classic models or the crown on Pixel Watch. The watch's vibration motor lets games give you taps on the wrist.

In Cozy Fishing you tap to cast and reel with a swipe or a turn of the crown. A session takes 15 to 40 seconds, which fits the watch well: a minute in a queue or a short break.

Do you need your phone nearby?

A Wear OS watch pairs with an Android phone for setup, but once a game is installed, many play on the watch alone. Games with online modes, leaderboards or downloadable content need a connection, and the Google Play description usually says so.

Cozy Fishing needs no internet and no account, so it plays the same on a plane, on a run or anywhere without signal.

How to remove a game from the watch

Open the watch's app list, then touch and hold the game and choose uninstall. You can also open the Play Store on the watch, find the game and uninstall it from its page.

Uninstalling from the watch doesn't remove the phone version of the same game, so delete that separately if you no longer want it.

Questions about games on Galaxy Watch

Can you play games on a Samsung watch?

Yes, on Galaxy Watch4 and newer. These watches run Wear OS and install games from Google Play. Older Tizen models such as Galaxy Watch3 can't install Google Play games.

How do I download games on my Galaxy Watch?

Open the Play Store on the watch, find the game and tap Install. Or open the game's Play Store page on your phone and choose your watch as the install target.

Why can't I find a game on my watch?

Either the game has no Wear OS version, or it's installed on the phone but not on the watch. Open the game's Play Store page on the phone and install it to the watch, or check the watch's Play Store for watch versions of your phone apps.

Are there free games for Galaxy Watch?

Yes. Many Wear OS games are free to download, some with optional in-app purchases. Cozy Fishing is free down to 10 m, with one optional one-time purchase that unlocks the deep rods.

Can I play watch games without the internet?

Many games work offline once installed. Games with online play need a connection. Cozy Fishing plays fully offline, with no account needed.
