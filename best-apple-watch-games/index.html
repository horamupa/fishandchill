Questions about Apple Watch games

Can you play games on Apple Watch?

Yes. Apple Watch installs its own apps, games included, from the App Store. Some games exist only on the watch, others are iPhone games with a watch version. Every game on this list plays on the wrist itself.

How do you get games on Apple Watch?

Open the App Store on the watch and search for a game. Or install it on iPhone, open the Watch app, go to My Watch, find the game under Available Apps and tap Install. With automatic app install turned on in the Watch app, watch versions of your iPhone apps appear on their own.

Are there free Apple Watch games?

Yes. Fish & Chill, Dare the Monkey, Hit The Island, Ping Pong, Wordfully, Solitaire The Game and Sudoku Puzzle are free to download. Some offer optional in-app purchases.

Do Apple Watch games need an iPhone nearby?

Once installed, the games on this list run on the watch. Some sync progress through their iPhone app when it's around. Fish & Chill plays fully on the watch and works offline.

Which Apple Watch do I need?

Each game lists its minimum watchOS version on its App Store page. Fish & Chill needs watchOS 11 or later.
