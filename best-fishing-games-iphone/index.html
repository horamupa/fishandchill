The best fishing games for iPhone in 2026

Mobile fishing games split into very different camps: realistic simulators with tackle boxes, competitive duels, story adventures, idle clickers and quiet lakes you visit for five minutes. These ten are the best mobile fishing games we found in each camp, and every one is free to download.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. We picked games where fishing is the core of the game, with an App Store rating of 4.5 or higher, that were on sale in the US App Store in October 2026.

  1. 1. Fish & Chill

    Cozy fishing · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.7 (11)

    A calm fishing game where the lake follows your real clock: dawn, day, dusk and night match the sky outside. Twenty species bite at their own hours, baits go from bread to glowworm, and the line upgrades from 10 to 200 m to reach the deep ones. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and on Apple Watch you reel with the Digital Crown without your iPhone.

    Download on the App Store

  2. 2. Fishing Clash: Sport Simulator

    Multiplayer sport fishing · Free · ★ 4.8 (158K)

    A sport fishing simulator with fisheries modeled on real places, from the Florida coast and the Kenai River to Lake Biwa and Loch Ness. You cast, then tap Strike to keep the line tension centered. Duels, championships and upgradable lure cards make it the competitive pick.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. Creatures of the Deep

    Fishing adventure · Free · ★ 4.8 (66K)

    Fishing mixed with exploration: paradise islands, haunted lakes and toxic wastelands hide more than 600 fish, creatures, treasures and legendary monsters. There are story characters, PvP duels, clans and a camp with an aquarium to build. Made by the studio behind Sky Force.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Fishing Planet

    Realistic simulator · Free · ★ 4.8 (22K)

    A first-person online fishing simulator built by anglers. More than 200 species react to season, weather, time of day and water temperature across 26 waterways based on real locations. Float, spinning, bottom and saltwater trolling, plus boats that fit up to four friends.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. Fishing Master

    3D casual fishing · Free · ★ 4.8 (24K)

    Realistic 3D spots like Caddo Lake, Key West and the Bering Sea, with simple controls: you cast with a motion of your phone. Over 100 species, a monster fishing mode for sharks and giant squid, and duels with friends for leaderboard places.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Tides: A Fishing Game

    Relaxing · Free · ★ 4.8 (22K)

    A small, meditative fishing game with serene visuals and calming music. You drive a boat to expedition spots, collect more than 50 fish, upgrade boats and expand your home island. A good pick if you want fishing without competition.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. Fishing Paradiso

    Story · Free · ★ 4.8 (2.8K)

    A narrative fishing game: a young boy wakes up on a tropical island in a heaven where fishing works a little differently. You complete fishing quests, discover more than 100 species and slowly uncover a mystery about a big fish. A sequel to Bear's Restaurant by Odencat.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. Fishing Travel

    Fishing and building · Free · ★ 4.7 (45K)

    A relaxed fishing trip across lakes, rivers and the open ocean, each with its own species. Harder spots pay better, so you combine and upgrade rods and tackle to match. Rewards go toward building your own place, from small items up to a mansion.

    View on the App Store

  9. 9. Ace Fishing: Wild Catch

    Arcade fishing · Free · ★ 4.6 (4.9K)

    One-touch controls make it easy to start, then you tire out big fish in long fights. Rods and lines are customizable by length and tension, the map covers famous spots around the world, and tournaments run every hour with global rankings.

    View on the App Store

  10. 10. Hooked Inc: Fishing Games

    Idle fishing · Free · ★ 4.7 (503K)

    An idle clicker: swipe to catch fish, earn money, hire a crew and upgrade your boat to unlock new seas. Your crew keeps fishing while you're away, so progress waits for you when you come back. Daily tournaments add a competitive layer.

    View on the App Store

How to choose a fishing game

Decide what you want from a session first. Realistic simulators like Fishing Planet and Fishing Clash reward knowing your tackle and fish behavior, and they lean on online play. Casual 3D games like Fishing Master and Ace Fishing keep the controls to a tap or a flick. Idle games like Hooked Inc progress even when you're not playing.

If you just want to unwind, look for small calm games: Tides, Fishing Paradiso or Fish & Chill. They have no duels to win and no rankings to chase.

Questions about fishing games on iPhone

What is the best fishing game for iPhone?

It depends on what you like. For realism, Fishing Planet. For competition, Fishing Clash. For adventure, Creatures of the Deep. For a calm break, Fish & Chill or Tides. For a story, Fishing Paradiso. For idle progress, Hooked Inc.

Are there free fishing games for iPhone?

Yes. All ten games on this list are free to download. Most offer optional in-app purchases; Fish & Chill has one optional one-time PRO purchase.

What is the most realistic mobile fishing game?

Of the games here, Fishing Planet goes furthest: its fish react to weather, season, time of day and water temperature, and its waterways are based on real locations. Fishing Clash is also realistic but more focused on duels.

Is there a fishing game for Apple Watch?

Yes. Fish & Chill plays fully on Apple Watch: you cast with a tap and reel by turning the Digital Crown, with haptic feedback. None of the other games on this list has an Apple Watch version.
