Games like Dave the Diver: 10 diving and fishing games for iPhone in 2026

Dave the Diver is the game where you dive into the Blue Hole by day, catch fish with a harpoon and serve them in a sushi restaurant by night. Since September 2026 it also runs on iPhone: the opening is free and a one-time purchase unlocks the rest. If you've finished it, or your phone can't run it, these ten games like Dave the Diver keep parts of the same loop: go under, bring something back, upgrade, go deeper.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest, and know that it's calm lake fishing, with no diving and no restaurant. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one shares diving, an underwater world or the catch, sell and upgrade loop with Dave the Diver, plays on iPhone and was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026.

  1. 1. Fish & Chill

    Calm fishing · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.8 (19)

    The quiet side of Dave the Diver: the catch and the chase for depth, without the combat or the dinner rush. The lake follows your real clock and fish keep their own biting hours, so the evening water holds different fish than the morning. You upgrade the line from 10 to 200 m and move from bread to glowworm bait. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and on Apple Watch you reel with the Digital Crown.

    Download on the App Store

  2. 2. DREDGE

    Dark fishing adventure · Paid · ★ 4.3 (1.1K)

    The closest in structure: fish by day, sell the catch, upgrade, and think twice about staying out after dark. You captain a trawler between remote islands, take quests from the locals and fit better gear to reach deep-sea trenches, with over 125 creatures to find. The first area is free, and a one-time purchase unlocks the base game.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. Dive & Sushi

    Dive and sushi · Free · ★ 4.2 (274)

    The same two halves as Dave the Diver in a lighter casual game. You dive with a harpoon, fight aggressive fish and pick random skill upgrades along the way, then turn the catch into sushi. On land you hire chefs and waiters, add dishes to the menu and spend the profit on oxygen tanks, diving suits and weapons. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. WellDiver

    Deep diving · Free · ★ 4.6 (431)

    Only the diving half, pushed further down. The deeper you go, the rarer the fish and minerals, and the harder it is to get back to the surface with them. Selling fish pays for upgrades, research opens deeper water, and there is a fish collection to complete. Released in October 2026.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. Dive & Fish

    Underwater action · Free · ★ 4.6 (459)

    A diving adventure with more fighting than cooking. You catch hundreds of fish types for your Fish Book, upgrade the suit, weapons and gadgets to stay under longer, and face a boss in each region. Between dives you travel from island to island and pick up side quests.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Subnautica

    Ocean survival · Paid · ★ 4.6 (1.2K)

    For players who liked the Blue Hole more than the restaurant: a whole alien ocean to explore. You craft equipment, pilot submarines, build bases and manage hunger, thirst and oxygen, or switch to Freedom or Creative mode and explore without those pressures. A paid game with no free part, and its App Store page lists which devices are supported.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. Creatures of the Deep

    Monster fishing · Free · ★ 4.8 (66K)

    Dave the Diver's collecting side as an online fishing game. There are more than 600 species of fish, creatures, treasures and legendary monsters, a camp to build and an aquarium that earns passive income from your best catches. It also has PvP duels, clans and seasonal challenges, so expect a busier game.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. Kuma Sushi Bar

    Sushi restaurant · Free · ★ 4.8 (24K)

    Only the restaurant half, and a gentle one. You help a small bear named Kumaki run the family sushi bar, hire animal staff, decorate the place and level it up by checking in every day. No diving and no danger, just the evening shift.

    View on the App Store

  9. 9. Feed the Deep

    Cave-diving roguelike · Free · ★ 4.7 (413)

    A dark roguelike from the designer of Fruit Ninja and Jetpack Joyride. Each expedition goes into a new, procedurally generated system of undersea caves, where you collect resources for upgrades while managing oxygen, darkness and hostile wildlife. You set the pace: dive fast, or go slowly and drop markers along the way.

    View on the App Store

  10. 10. SILT

    Underwater puzzle adventure · Paid · ★ 4.8 (4.3K)

    The eerie depths without fishing or cooking. You play a lone diver in a hand-drawn, black-and-white abyss and possess sea creatures to solve puzzles and get past giant deep-sea beasts. You can try it for free, and a purchase unlocks the full game.

    View on the App Store

Ratings: US App Store, November 2026.

Questions about games like Dave the Diver

What games are like Dave the Diver?

On iPhone: Dive & Sushi for the same dive-and-restaurant loop, DREDGE for fishing by day and danger at night, WellDiver and Dive & Fish for diving with upgrades, Subnautica for a full ocean to explore, and Kuma Sushi Bar for the restaurant alone. For calm fishing, Fish & Chill.

Is Dave the Diver on iPhone?

Yes. Dave the Diver came to the App Store in September 2026, published by MintRocket. Its App Store page describes it as a full port of the PC game. In November 2026 the page lists English as its only language.

Is Dave the Diver free on mobile?

The download is free and lets you play the opening portion. A one-time purchase unlocks the full game, and DLC expansions are sold separately as in-app purchases.

Which iPhones can run Dave the Diver?

The App Store page says the game needs a device with 6 GB of RAM or more: iPhone 12 Pro and 12 Pro Max, iPhone 13 Pro and 13 Pro Max, iPhone 14 or newer. On other iPhones, the lighter games on this list are the way in.

Is there a relaxing game like Dave the Diver?

Kuma Sushi Bar keeps only the restaurant and has no danger. Fish & Chill keeps only the fishing: a lake on your real clock, no combat, no ads and no timers, and it works offline.
