The best relaxing games for iPhone in 2026

Relaxing games are the ones you open when your head is full: soft sound, a slow pace and nothing that punishes you for stopping. These ten calm games on iPhone cover everything from a quiet lake and a koi pond to color puzzles and a box of stress relief toys.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. We picked games with a slow pace and calming sound or visuals, rated 4.5 or higher, on sale in the US App Store in October 2026. Apple Arcade and Netflix games are left out because they need a subscription.

  1. 1. Fish & Chill

    Calm fishing · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.8 (12)

    A quiet lake that runs on your real clock, with soft water and slow waves. You cast, wait for a bite and reel in, and each of the twenty species keeps its own hours. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and on Apple Watch you reel with the Digital Crown without your iPhone.

    Download on the App Store

  2. 2. Zen Koi 2

    Koi pond · Free · ★ 4.8 (30K)

    Swim a koi through a calm pond, collect smaller fish and breed new colors until your koi turn into dragons. Calming music plays throughout, and you can decorate your own underwater zen garden.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. I Love Hue

    Color puzzle · Free · ★ 4.9 (50K)

    Rearrange mosaics of colored tiles until they form a perfect gradient. It's a puzzle of perception rather than logic, with a soothing synth soundtrack and more than 900 levels.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Flow Free

    Line puzzle · Free · ★ 4.8 (408K)

    Connect matching dots with pipes until the whole board is filled. Free Play has no clock, so you can solve at your own pace; Time Trial is there if you want pressure. Over 2,500 free levels and a new daily puzzle.

    View on the App Store

    Idle tapper · Free · ★ 4.7 (9.3K)

    Tap the screen to collect hearts, bring animals to your island and grow a small oasis to calming music. It's built around relaxation: no complex controls and no stressful goals.

    Self-care idle · Free · ★ 4.9 (11K)

    Catch shy creatures that stand for feelings like anxiety or loneliness, then grow each one into a unique plant. You water and care for them, play small mini-games and slowly turn a dusty room into a safe space.

    Interactive story · Paid · ★ 4.6 (8.8K)

    A short story about a first love, from meeting to growing apart, told through small mini-game vignettes inspired by graphic novels. It comes from the lead designer of Monument Valley. Some chapters are sad, so it's quiet rather than cheerful.

    View on the App Store

How to pick a relaxing game

Look for three things: you can stop at any moment without losing progress, there's no timer by default, and the sound is soft. Every game on this list meets the first two, though Flow Free and Mini Metro also offer timed or harder modes you can skip.

Then choose by mood. If you want your hands busy and your mind empty, try Antistress, Flow Free or I Love Hue. If you want to watch something grow, Zen Koi 2, My Oasis or Dustbunny. If you'd rather wait for a quiet moment, Fish & Chill's lake follows the time of day outside. Games can help you unwind, but they aren't a treatment for anxiety.

Questions about relaxing games on iPhone

What are the most relaxing games on iPhone?

For calm puzzles, I Love Hue, Flow Free and Hidden Folks. For something to grow, Zen Koi 2, My Oasis and Dustbunny. For a quiet lake, Fish & Chill. For a short story, Florence.

Are there free relaxing games?

Yes. Fish & Chill, Zen Koi 2, I Love Hue, Flow Free, My Oasis, Antistress and Dustbunny are free to download. Most offer optional in-app purchases.

What are good stress relief games?

Antistress gives you 50 small fidget toys for a one-minute break. My Oasis is built specifically around relaxation, and Dustbunny is about caring for your feelings as plants. They can help you unwind, but they aren't therapy.

Is there a relaxing game for Apple Watch?

Yes. Fish & Chill plays fully on Apple Watch: you cast with a tap and reel by turning the Digital Crown, with haptics for every bite. The other games on this list are for iPhone and iPad.
