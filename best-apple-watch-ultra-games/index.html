The best Apple Watch Ultra games are the ones that use that hardware: arcade collections with room for your fingers, and games you steer by turning the crown. Here are nine, checked in November 2026.

What's different about games on Apple Watch Ultra

Apple Watch Ultra runs the same watchOS apps as every other Apple Watch, so there is no separate Ultra game catalog: any watch game installs on it. What changes is the hardware. Ultra has the largest display in the lineup, a bigger Digital Crown and the longest battery life of any Apple Watch.

That helps in three ways. Buttons and small text get more room, which matters in game collections and strategy. Games steered with the crown, like fishing, pong, mini golf and shooters, are easier to control with a bigger crown. And a larger battery leaves more room for a few rounds a day.

Questions about Apple Watch Ultra games

Can you play games on Apple Watch Ultra?

Yes. Apple Watch Ultra installs games from the App Store like any other Apple Watch, and they run on the watch itself. Every game on this list has its own Apple Watch app.

Are there games made only for Apple Watch Ultra?

We didn't find any. Ultra uses the same watchOS and the same App Store as other Apple Watch models, so developers ship one watch app for all of them. Some, like Arcadia and Arcadia Sports, name Ultra support on their App Store page.

Are there free Apple Watch Ultra games?

Yes. Fish & Chill, Ping Pong, Mini Golf and Hit The Island are free to download. Some free games offer optional in-app purchases; the App Store page lists them under In-App Purchases.

How do you download games on Apple Watch Ultra?

Press the Digital Crown, open the App Store on the watch and search for the game. Or install it on your iPhone, open the Watch app, go to My Watch, find the game under Available Apps and tap Install.

Does Fish & Chill work on Apple Watch Ultra?

Fish & Chill needs watchOS 11 or later, and every Apple Watch Ultra can run it. The whole game plays on the watch without your iPhone and works offline.
