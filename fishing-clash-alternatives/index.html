Games like Fishing Clash: 10 alternatives for iPhone in 2026

Fishing Clash by Ten Square Games is one of the biggest fishing games on mobile: 3D spots around the world, lure cards to upgrade, duels and championships against other players. If you want something similar, or the same fishing with less competition, these ten games like Fishing Clash cover both: deeper simulators, other PvP fishing games, and quiet ones you can play offline.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one is built around fishing, plays on iPhone and was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026. Let's Fish comes from Ten Square Games, the same studio as Fishing Clash, and is marked as such.

  1. 1. Fish & Chill

    Calm fishing on your clock · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.8 (12)

    The opposite of a duel: one quiet lake that follows your real time of day, so dusk outside means dusk on the water. Each of the 20 species keeps its own biting hours, the line upgrades from 10 to 200 m, and baits run from bread to glowworm. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and on Apple Watch you reel with the Digital Crown.

    Download on the App Store

  2. 2. Fishing Planet

    Realistic simulator · Free · ★ 4.8 (22K)

    A first-person online simulator for players who want more realism than Fishing Clash. Over 200 species react to season, weather, time of day and water temperature across 26 waterways based on real places. Float, spinning, bottom and saltwater trolling, boats you can share with up to three friends, and online tournaments.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. Creatures of the Deep

    Fishing adventure · Free · ★ 4.8 (66K)

    Fishing with a story: rumors, shadows under the water and legendary monsters to hunt across paradise islands, haunted lakes and toxic wastelands. More than 600 species, PvP duels, clans and seasonal challenges, plus a camp and an aquarium to build up. From Infinite Dreams, the studio behind Sky Force.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Ace Fishing: Wild Catch

    Tournaments · Free · ★ 4.6 (4.9K)

    One of the older 3D sport fishing games, from Com2uS. One-touch controls, rods tuned by line length and strength, and hundreds of fish spread over famous spots around the world. Hourly tournaments and head-to-head matches against players worldwide give it the same competitive pull as Fishing Clash.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. Fishing Master

    Casual 3D · Free · ★ 4.8 (24K)

    Realistic-looking spots such as Caddo Lake, Key West and the Bering Sea, with more than 100 species from bass to monster sharks and squid. You cast with a motion gesture, so it feels close to the real thing. Duel friends for leaderboard places and join monster raids during events.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Fishing Blitz!

    1v1 duels · Free · ★ 4.8 (11K)

    Start at Antler Lake and unlock places like Bahia Honda, Las Vueltas and Oyster Bay, each with its own fish. Boosts such as Luck, Weight and Sonar raise your chance of a rare catch. Missions and 1v1 duels against friends make it the closest in structure to Fishing Clash on this list.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. Monster Fishing 2026

    Offline 3D · Free · ★ 4.5 (2.8K)

    A full 3D fishing game with more than 250 species across 30 well-known spots. The App Store page says it needs no internet connection and supports automatic fishing, so it works on a plane or in a cabin with no signal.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. Master Bass Angler: Fishing

    Bass tournaments · Free · ★ 4.7 (3.6K)

    An arcade game only for bass: seven bass species on ten lakes, with swipe-to-cast controls and fast catches. Free daily tournaments and head-to-head play cover the competitive side. It needs an internet connection, and ads can be removed with an in-app purchase.

    View on the App Store

  9. 9. Let's Fish: Sport Fishing Games

    Same studio · Free · ★ 4.4 (8.6K)

    An earlier sport fishing game from Ten Square Games, the studio behind Fishing Clash. Summer and winter fishing, ice and fly fishing, and friends to compete against. Worth a look if you like how Fishing Clash plays and want different spots.

    View on the App Store

  10. 10. Tides: A Fishing Game

    Calm boat trips · Free · ★ 4.8 (22K)

    A small, meditative fishing game with soft visuals and calm music. You sail your boat to different destinations, collect more than 50 fish, upgrade boats and grow your home island. No duels, no championships, just fishing.

    View on the App Store

Pick by what you want from Fishing Clash

For more realism, start with Fishing Planet. For duels and tournaments, try Ace Fishing, Fishing Blitz! or Master Bass Angler. For fishing with a story and monsters, Creatures of the Deep. To play with no signal, Monster Fishing 2026 and Fish & Chill.

And if what you actually wanted was the calm of fishing without the race for ranks, Tides and Fish & Chill skip the competition entirely.

Ratings: US App Store, November 2026.

Questions about Fishing Clash alternatives

What games are like Fishing Clash?

On iPhone: Fishing Planet for a deeper simulator, Ace Fishing, Fishing Blitz! and Fishing Master for duels and tournaments, Creatures of the Deep for fishing with monsters and clans, and Let's Fish from the same studio. For calm fishing without PvP, Tides and Fish & Chill.

Who makes Fishing Clash?

Fishing Clash: Sport Simulator is made by Ten Square Games, a Polish studio. It also makes Let's Fish, an earlier sport fishing game that is still on the App Store.

Is there a fishing game like Fishing Clash that works offline?

Yes. Monster Fishing 2026 says on its App Store page that it needs no internet connection. Fish & Chill also works fully offline, on iPhone and on Apple Watch.

Is there a relaxing fishing game without PvP?

Tides is a small, meditative game about sailing and collecting fish. Fish & Chill is a single quiet lake on your real clock, with no ads and no energy timers. Neither has duels or rankings to chase.

Can you play Fishing Clash on Apple Watch?

No, its App Store page lists no Apple Watch app. If you want to fish on your wrist, Fish & Chill runs fully on Apple Watch, and you reel with the Digital Crown.
