Questions about Galaxy Watch and Wear OS games

Can you play games on a Galaxy Watch?

Yes. Galaxy Watch4 and newer run Wear OS and install games from Google Play, and so does Pixel Watch. Some games exist only on the watch, others come with a phone app. Every game on this list plays on the watch itself.

How do you get games on a Wear OS watch?

Open the Play Store on the watch and search for a game. Or open the game's page in the Play Store on your phone, choose your watch as the install target and install it there.

Are there free Galaxy Watch games?

Yes. Cozy Fishing, Wrist Chess, Games In One, 2048 for smart watch, Solitaire Wearable, Magic Cube and Watchsteroids are free to download. Some offer optional in-app purchases.

Do Wear OS games need a phone nearby?

Once installed, the games here run on the watch. Cozy Fishing needs no internet, Games In One plays offline, and Wrist Chess needs a connection only for online games on Lichess.
