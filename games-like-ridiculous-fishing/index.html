Games like Ridiculous Fishing: 10 arcade fishing games for iPhone in 2026

Ridiculous Fishing by Vlambeer is the arcade fishing game where you drop a line as deep as it goes, hook everything on the way up, then shoot the catch out of the sky. Today its 3D remake, Ridiculous Fishing EX, needs an Apple Arcade subscription, and the 2013 original hasn't been updated since 2017. These ten games like Ridiculous Fishing keep the loop of going deeper, catching more and upgrading, and none of them needs a subscription to start.

We make Fish & Chill, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest, and know that it is calm fishing, not an arcade game. Every other game here comes from other developers, and nobody paid to be included. Each one is built around catching fish, plays on iPhone without Apple Arcade and was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026.

  1. 1. Fish & Chill

    Calm, not arcade · Free · optional one-time PRO · ★ 4.8 (18)

    The slow answer to Ridiculous Fishing: no guns and no chainsaws, just a lake that follows your real clock and fish that keep their own biting hours. What carries over is the chase for depth: you upgrade the line from 10 to 200 m and move from bread to glowworm bait to reach what lives lower. No ads, no energy timers, works offline, and on Apple Watch you reel with the Digital Crown.

  2. 2. Ninja Fishing

    Cast, reel, slice · Free · ★ 4.1 (1.1K)

    The closest match to the Ridiculous Fishing loop. You cast the hook as deep as you can, tilt the phone to gather fish on the way up, then slice them in mid-air with a katana while avoiding the dynamite. Gold buys katanas, boats and other upgrades, and there are over 140 species to collect across 6 islands. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  3. 3. Go Fish!

    Casual dive · Free · ★ 4.5 (455K)

    The same descent without the shooting: drop the line as deep as it goes and catch as many fish as you can on the way back up. Upgrades take you deeper and let you carry more, and an old fisherman keeps earning while you're away. A paid weekly VIP subscription removes the non-optional ads.

    View on the App Store

  4. 4. Fishing Food

    Silly and cute · Free · ★ 4.9 (54K)

    It shares the humor more than the guns: your cat fishes talking food out of the ocean. Tap to drop the line, swipe left and right to aim, and the catch flies up once the hook is full. Coins go into gear that reaches deeper, and its App Store page promises no forced ads. The last update was in July 2024. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  5. 5. The Fishercat

    Harpoon action · Free · ★ 4.8 (7.1K)

    For the part of Ridiculous Fishing where you shoot the fish. You play a cat with a harpoon: drag to aim, then fire at fish that never stop moving. There are over 150 kinds to collect, different harpoons and support items to unlock, and an aquarium to decorate with your catch.

    View on the App Store

  6. 6. Rule with an Iron Fish

    Pirate fishing RPG · Paid · ★ 4.8 (537)

    The same joke-filled fishing, stretched into an adventure. You're a pirate captain fishing in swamps, active volcanoes and the sky, catching 150 hand-painted fish and growing an island town with an aquarium, farm and restaurant. Like the original Ridiculous Fishing, you pay once: no in-app purchases and no ads. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  7. 7. DAVE THE DIVER

    Dive and sushi · Paid · ★ 4.2 (367)

    Dive into the Blue Hole by day, catching fish with a harpoon and other weapons, and run a sushi restaurant by night. Profits go into better gear for deeper dives, and running out of oxygen means leaving your haul behind. The opening is free to try, and a one-time purchase unlocks the full game. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  8. 8. DREDGE

    Dark fishing adventure · Paid · ★ 4.3 (1.1K)

    Go deeper, catch stranger things, upgrade the boat: the Ridiculous Fishing loop turned into a slow, eerie adventure. You captain a trawler between remote islands, sell your catch, take on quests and fit better equipment to reach deep-sea trenches, with over 125 creatures to find. The first area is free, and a one-time purchase unlocks the base game.

  9. 9. Fishing Break

    Casual collection · Free · ★ 4.7 (13K)

    Quick casts and a fight with every fish: 34 spots across 12 worlds, hundreds of species and rare elemental variants for your aquarium museum. Coins buy better rods, reels, hooks and bait, every fish has its own leaderboard, and its App Store page says there are no energy timers. Only in English.

    View on the App Store

  10. 10. Creatures of the Deep

    Monster fishing · Free · ★ 4.8 (66K)

    For players whose favorite part was the odd catches: more than 600 species of fish, creatures, treasures and legendary monsters, in places that range from paradise islands to haunted lakes. It's an online game with PvP duels, clans and seasonal challenges, so it asks for more time than a quick Ridiculous Fishing run.

Which game is closest to Ridiculous Fishing?

Ninja Fishing is the closest: it has the same three steps of dropping the line, reeling fish up and destroying them in the air. For the descent and the upgrades without the shooting, pick Go Fish! or Fishing Food. For shooting at fish, The Fishercat.

If you liked the humor and a game you buy once, try Rule with an Iron Fish. For a bigger adventure built on the same go-deeper loop, DAVE THE DIVER or DREDGE. And if you'd rather slow down completely, Fish & Chill and Fishing Break let you take one fish at a time.

Questions about games like Ridiculous Fishing

What games are like Ridiculous Fishing?

On iPhone: Ninja Fishing for the same cast, reel and slice loop, Go Fish! and Fishing Food for deep dives with upgrades, The Fishercat for harpoon action, and Rule with an Iron Fish for comic pirate fishing. For bigger adventures, DAVE THE DIVER and DREDGE. For calm fishing, Fish & Chill.

Is Ridiculous Fishing still on the App Store?

Yes, in two versions. Ridiculous Fishing EX, the 3D remake, is part of Apple Arcade. The original Ridiculous Fishing – A Tale of Redemption is still sold for $2.99 in the US App Store, but its last update was in August 2017.

Do you need Apple Arcade to play Ridiculous Fishing?

For Ridiculous Fishing EX, yes: it's an Apple Arcade game. The 2013 original is a separate one-time purchase. None of the ten games on this list needs Apple Arcade.

Who made Ridiculous Fishing?

Vlambeer, together with Zach Gage and Greg Wohlwend. The original won an Apple Design Award in 2013. The EX remake was made in collaboration with KO_OP.

Is there a game like Ridiculous Fishing without ads?

Rule with an Iron Fish is a one-time purchase with no ads and no in-app purchases. Fish & Chill is free and has no ads. Fishing Food says on its App Store page that it has no forced ads.
